Finish Strong.

Most men do not fall. They drift. This is a ninety-minute plan to close out the year with order: score yourself, pick one goal, and leave with a 90-day plan broken into weekly targets.

Renatus Vincere · Reborn to overcome
Step 1

Assess

20 min

Score each area from 1 (broken) to 10 (dialed in). Go with your gut, not your intentions.

Three honest questions

Step 2

Focus

15 min

What I’m stopping until January 1

Low-margin jobs. Late-night scrolling. Meetings with no outcome. Chasing every lead. Admin a tool could handle. Saying yes to things you resent.





Step 3

Plan

30 min

Work backward. Where do you need to be at the end of each phase?

Weekly targets

One result you can check off by Friday. Holiday weeks are flagged — plan them light. Those weeks are for the people you’re doing this for.

WeekStartsTarget

Obstacles



Step 4

Rhythm

10 min

Daily 3-3-1. Three minutes each morning to pick today’s one move and do it before email. Three minutes each night to check it and set tomorrow’s. Plus one non-negotiable — the thing you do when you have nothing left.

Friday 15. Every Friday, fifteen minutes: Did I hit the target? What worked? What’s next week’s target? Which area got neglected?

Text him your goal today and your Friday score every week. Shame’s whole power supply is secrecy. This is how you cut it.
