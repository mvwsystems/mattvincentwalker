Privacy Policy
Effective November 26, 2026

This policy explains what information Superhuman LLC (“we,” “us”) collects through mattvincentwalker.com and its pages, including our product pages (the “Site”), how we use it, and the choices you have. We collect as little as we need, and we do not sell your personal information.

What we collect

Information you give us. When you subscribe to a newsletter, take an assessment, request a plan or report, apply for coaching, or send a speaking or consulting inquiry, we collect what you enter: usually your name and email address, plus any answers or messages you choose to share.

Purchase information. When you buy a product, we collect your name, email address, and order details so we can deliver your downloads and support you. You enter your payment details into a secure form run by Stripe, our payment processor, which handles the payment. We never see or store your full card number.

Technical information. Like any website, our hosting provider automatically records basic request data such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you request, for security and to keep the Site running. We do not currently run advertising pixels or analytics cookies on the Site. Our checkout page loads Stripe’s payment form, which may set its own cookies to prevent fraud, under Stripe’s privacy policy.

How we use it

  • To deliver what you asked for: products, downloads, reports, plans, and newsletters.
  • To reply to your messages and review applications and inquiries.
  • To send email you signed up for. Every email includes an unsubscribe link.
  • To provide customer support, process refunds, and keep records required by law.
  • To protect the Site against fraud, abuse, and security threats.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers who help us run the Site, and only as needed for them to do that work:

  • Netlify, which hosts the Site and runs its forms.
  • Beehiiv and Kit, which manage our email lists.
  • Resend, which sends confirmation and delivery emails.
  • Stripe, which processes payments and helps prevent fraud.
  • Anthropic, whose AI may help us read and summarize written application answers. Anthropic does not use information sent through its business API to train its models.
  • Google Fonts, which serves the Site’s typefaces and receives your IP address when a page loads.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect our rights or someone’s safety, or as part of a sale or transfer of our business.

Advertising on other platforms

We may advertise on platforms such as Facebook and Instagram. If you reach the Site from one of those ads, the platform’s own privacy policy governs what it collects on its side. This Site is not part of, or endorsed by, Meta Platforms, Inc.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as you stay subscribed or as long as we need it to provide what you asked for, then delete it, except where we must keep records for tax, accounting, or legal reasons.

Your choices and rights

You can unsubscribe from any email at any time using the link at the bottom of the message. You can also ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it by emailing mvw@mattvincentwalker.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and we will honor them.

Security

We use reputable providers and reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page.
