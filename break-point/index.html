The Break Point

WHERE YOU’LL
BREAK

And what you’re actually standing on.

Every man breaks somewhere. Fifteen honest answers. Three minutes. This tells you where — before the pressure does.

No result makes you look good. That’s the point.

Two Rules
01

Answer as you actually behave, not as you intend to.

Your last 90 days of conduct are the only admissible evidence.

02

If two answers feel true, pick the uglier one.

The flattering answer is almost always the defended one.
