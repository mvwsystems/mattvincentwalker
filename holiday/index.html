#6

Custom Packing List Generator

Use it when: Before any holiday trip. This is the prompt that inspired this whole pack.

Make a printable packing checklist for each person on our trip. Trip: [DESTINATION], [NUMBER] nights, leaving [DATE]. Travelers: [NAME, AGE] for each person. Weather will be [FORECAST OR CLIMATE]. Activities: [LIST, LIKE HIKING, A NICE DINNER, SWIMMING, CHURCH]. We are driving / flying and luggage space is [DETAILS]. Give each person their own list with quantities for every item, grouped into clothes, shoes and outerwear, toiletries, gear, and comfort items. Add three columns next to every item: Have, Need to Buy, Packed. Finish with one shared family list (chargers, first aid, snacks, documents) and a short do-not-forget list of the items people most often leave behind for this kind of trip.

PRO TIPPrint one copy per person. Run the Have column today, shop the Buy column tomorrow, check Packed as it goes in the bag.
